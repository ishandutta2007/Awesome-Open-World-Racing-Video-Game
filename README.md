## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Forza Horizon 5](https://forza.net/)**  

  **The definitive open-world racing game** — Mexico's diverse landscapes, 500+ cars, seasonal events, and the best arcade-sim handling. **The reference point for open-world racing** .



- **[The Crew Motorfest](https://www.ubisoft.com/game/the-crew-motorfest)**  

  **Ubisoft's open-world racing playground** — Hawaii's O'ahu island, diverse vehicle types (cars, bikes, boats, planes), and themed playlists. **The most vehicle-diverse open-world racer** .



- **[Need for Speed Unbound](https://www.ea.com/games/need-for-speed/need-for-speed-unbound)**  

  **Street racing with graffiti art style** — Lakeshore City, intense police chases, and deep customization. **The best modern street racing game** .



- **[Burnout Paradise Remastered](https://www.ea.com/games/burnout/burnout-paradise-remastered)**  

  **The classic open-world arcade racer** — Paradise City, crash modes, and stunt runs. **The gold standard for arcade open-world racing** .



- **[Test Drive Unlimited Solar Crown](https://testdriveunlimited.ubisoft.com/)**  

  **Open-world racing with Hong Kong Island** — luxury cars, social hubs, and exploration. **The most social open-world racer** .



- **[Midnight Club: Los Angeles](https://www.rockstargames.com/midnightclub)**  

  **The classic open-world street racer** — Los Angeles, illegal racing, and police chases. **Historically significant** .



- **[Lego 2K Drive](https://lego2kdrive.com/)**  

  **Open-world Lego racing** — Bricklandia, vehicle customization, and family-friendly fun. **The best open-world racer for families** .



- **[Grid Legends](https://www.ea.com/games/grid/grid-legends)**  

  **Racing with a story mode** — professional circuits and stadium events. **Not open-world** but included for its diverse racing content.



- **[Fuel](https://www.codemasters.com/)**  

  **The largest open-world racing game ever made** — 5,000 square miles of post-apocalyptic terrain. **Historically significant for map scale** .



- **[Driver: San Francisco](https://www.ubisoft.com/game/driver-san-francisco)**  

  **The classic open-world driving game** — San Francisco, Shift mechanic, and story-driven missions. **The best open-world driving narrative** .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Speed Dreams](https://github.com/speed-dreams/speed-dreams)**  

  **The leading open-source racing simulator**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Fork of TORCS with modern graphics, improved physics, and active development** — 100+ cars, 100+ tracks . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux) . **The most complete open-source racing sim** — 20+ years of lineage from TORCS . **Best for players wanting a full open-source racing experience** .



- **[Stunt Rally](https://github.com/stuntrally/stuntrally)**  

  **Open-source rally and stunt driving game with 3D tracks**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **3D tracks with loops, jumps, and obstacles** — physics-based driving . **The most fun open-source racing game** — arcade-style with simulation elements . **Supports open-world exploration** in some modes . **Best for players wanting rally and stunt gameplay** .



- **[VDrift](https://github.com/vdrift/vdrift)**  

  **Open-source drift racing simulator**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Focus on drift physics and realistic car handling** . **The best open-source drift-specific sim** . **Best for drift enthusiasts** .



- **[Trigger Rally](https://github.com/TriggerRally/server)**  

  **Open-source rally game in the browser**, MIT licensed . **Lightweight and accessible** — runs in any modern browser . **The easiest entry to open-source racing** — no installation required . **Best for quick browser-based rally fun** .



- **[TORCS](https://github.com/fmirus/torcs-1.3.7)**  

  **The Open Racing Car Simulator — the original open-source racing sim**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **The foundation for Speed Dreams** — historically significant . **Mature but dated** — best for understanding racing sim architecture . **Best for historical interest and engine study** .



- **[SuperTuxKart](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code)**  

  **Open-source kart racing game**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **4,000+ GitHub stars** . **Mario Kart-style racing** — 20+ tracks, 20+ characters, and multiplayer . **The most complete open-source kart racer** . **Best for family-friendly open-source racing** .



- **[Rigs of Rods](https://github.com/RigsOfRods/rigs-of-rods)**  

  **Open-source vehicle simulation sandbox**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Soft-body physics** for realistic vehicle deformation . **Build and drive vehicles** — trucks, cars, planes, and boats . **The best open-source physics sandbox** . **Open-world maps available** . **Best for players wanting vehicle engineering sandbox** .



- **[OpenTTD](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD)**  

  **Open-source transport simulation RTS**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **7,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not racing** — but open-world transport management . **Best for players wanting economic open-world gameplay** .



- **[OpenRCT2](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2)**  

  **Open-source reimplementation of RollerCoaster Tycoon 2**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not racing** — but open-world management with ride construction . **Best for theme park fans** .

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Open-world racing games often support mods and custom content. **Verify mod sources** — malicious mods can compromise systems. Use official repositories and community-vetted sources.

- **Open-source racing sims vary in polish** — Speed Dreams is complete but dated graphically; Stunt Rally is fun but arcade-focused; VDrift is niche . Evaluate against your expectations.

- **Force feedback support varies** — commercial sims (Forza, The Crew) have superior force feedback implementation. Open-source sims may not support all wheels or provide the same fidelity .

- **Some open-source racing games require original game assets** — OpenRCT2 requires RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 files. It is an engine reimplementation, not a full game distribution .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong physics, track, and vehicle foundations, but **massive open worlds, licensed vehicles, and professional force-feedback fidelity** remain primarily commercial offerings.
